Model performance

Evaluation on the held-out temporal test set (2023–2025) across five Indian cities.

Recall and precision are the most important metrics for an early-warning system. High recall means fewer missed heatwave days (false negatives); high precision means fewer false alarms. This model is tuned for recall—it catches most heatwave days at the cost of some false alarms.
